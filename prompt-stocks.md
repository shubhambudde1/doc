### 14. Important Rules

1. Use the **latest available information**.
2. Mention the **date** of important data.
3. Do not rely on a single news source.
4. Distinguish **facts from assumptions**.
5. Do not treat analyst opinions as facts.
6. Do not recommend a stock solely because its price has increased.
7. Look for catalysts that can cause future earnings or valuation changes.
8. Compare stocks with their sector peers.
9. Highlight contradictory signals.
10. If reliable data is unavailable, explicitly say so.
11. Do not fabricate prices, financial numbers, news, or events.
12. Give source links for important claims.
13. Prioritize information that can actually **move the stock price**.
14. Separate **long-term investment opportunities** from **short-term swing-trading opportunities**.
